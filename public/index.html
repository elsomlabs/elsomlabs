06 Software

One of the tools we've shipped

Case study · Latest build

RoutingLab

Dispatch and routing platform for fire protection

RoutingLab is a proprietary platform built for a fire protection company. It automates technician scheduling by evaluating skill proficiency, certifications, and location, then assigns each job to the technician best qualified for it and keeps their routes efficient.

  • SchedulingJobs are matched to technicians based on skill level, certifications, and proximity, replacing manual assignment.
  • Technician trackingGPS location and job clock-in and clock-out times are recorded, giving the office a clear view of where each technician is and what they are working on.
  • Route optimisationAddresses are entered into a custom map tool, which uses live traffic data to order the stops and create the most efficient route.
  • Time savingsSince deployment, RoutingLab's scheduling feature alone has saved the business approximately 15 hours of labour per week: 12 hours of scheduling and dispatch work for office staff and 3 hours of drive time and idle time for technicians.
